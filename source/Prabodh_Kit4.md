lesson we are going to learn certain sentence structures in which subject is followed
by 'को' case-ending. In these structures, the verb agrees with the object in gender &
number.

If there is no object in the sentence the verb will be always masculine singular e.g.
Subject + को Construction

The game of cricket:

There are two teams in a game of cricket. One team bowls (गें दबाजी करना) and
